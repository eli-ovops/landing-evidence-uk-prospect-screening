### Honest static-page commercial increment

`H0.5: PASS — MEANINGFUL COMMERCIAL ONE-PAGE OPPORTUNITY VERIFIED` (inherited candidate conclusion, reinforced by this research).

An owned, static, location-led page could make the relationship between the everyday cafe offer (breakfast/lunch), bookable/occasion-led offer (afternoon tea and bistro evenings), dietary range, local-ingredient story and practical visit route legible in one shareable destination. It should leave reservations / ordering to the existing external route; no live menu, inventory, payment, user account, or self-hosted booking is required for this bounded proposition.

This is a plausible `United Kingdom / GBP / £99 AS-SHOWN HANDOVER or £199 ONE PERSONALISED UPDATE` purchase thesis, not an offer, prediction, score, Demo instruction, or evidence of willingness to buy.

### Known limits and risks

- Menu, specials, bistro dates, prices, hours and availability are volatile; do not turn review content into a current menu or promise.
- Public Gmail on TripAdvisor is a business-contact fact only. Delivery status, preferred recipient, PECR / UK GDPR subscriber classification, lawful basis, opt-out history and sending authorisation are `UNKNOWN`.
- Company officers / PSC are public registry facts, not a licence to use private personal details or assume decision-maker reach.
- Traveller images and reviews are third-party/user-generated; they are **not** cleared customer assets and must not be harvested or reused for a Concept Demo.
- The Facebook URL may be official but was not accessible enough to establish ownership, content provenance, logo, images, current posts or licensing.
