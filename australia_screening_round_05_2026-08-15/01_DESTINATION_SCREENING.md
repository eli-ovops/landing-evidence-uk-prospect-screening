# 01 — destination selection

Retrieved: 2026-08-15 (Asia/Shanghai). This is a desk-research choice, not a demand or revenue claim.

| Place considered | Why it was considered | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fremantle, WA | A dense independent creative economy was visible in public results, but sampled photographers and studios already showed polished, current sites or commerce-heavy needs. | Not selected. |
| Beechworth, VIC | Historic tourism town with independent hospitality and arts, but likely concentration in accommodation, retail and events raises recurring availability/inventory risk. | Not selected. |
| Bellingen, NSW | Creative small-town profile, but initial public discovery was too thin for a compliant manual 15-business screen within this round. | Not selected. |
| **Kiama / Gerringong / nearby Foxground and Gerroa, NSW** | The current [Destination Kiama 2026 visitor guide](https://kiama.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/DK-2026-Visitor-Guide_FINAL-1.pdf) lists a cluster of named independent photographers, artists, galleries, ceramic studios and celebrants with their own URLs and phone contacts. This permits a manual, public 15-business screen while avoiding the prohibited locations. | **Selected.** |

The selected geography is treated as one small tourism-service area, not as proof that every business serves only Kiama.
