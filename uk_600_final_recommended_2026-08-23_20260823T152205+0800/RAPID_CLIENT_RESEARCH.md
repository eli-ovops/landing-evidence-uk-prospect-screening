# Rapid client research

## Wild Clover Skin Care — RECOMMEND

- Honest acquisition improvement: give search/social visitors an owned introduction to the specialist facial approach, compare three core treatments, show location and verified trust, then hand off to the existing booking system.
- Static feasibility: Hero, specialist positioning, three treatment summaries, 151-review trust statement, location/contact and one external Book CTA; no checkout, voucher, customer account or booking implementation.
- Real public basis: official logo/hero, official treatment descriptions and prices, current review evidence, public location and email. Any customer imagery remains research-only until separate Demo/material authorization.
- GBP600 rationale: published GBP65–GBP100 treatments and sustained review volume make a one-time owned acquisition page commercially explainable without asserting revenue or purchase probability.
- Risk control: no result promises, medical claims or invented testimonials; preserve Ovatu for booking and vouchers.
- Verdict: `RECOMMEND`.

## Tranquil Touch Relaxation Therapy — RECOMMEND

- Honest acquisition improvement: explain an unfamiliar ASMR massage/head-spa proposition before asking visitors to book, distinguish three core experiences and add reassurance from current reviews and the studio context.
- Static feasibility: Hero, what ASMR/head spa means, three service choices, who each suits, verified trust/location and one external booking CTA.
- Real public basis: official logo/studio image, official service descriptions, current dated reviews, address, email and social routes.
- GBP600 rationale: current review volume, dedicated branded treatment space, gift cards and paid booking tooling show meaningful presentation investment; the page supports a distinctive local-search decision.
- Risk control: avoid therapeutic/medical outcome claims; use only the business's factual service descriptions and external booking.
- Verdict: `RECOMMEND`.

## Lavella Hair Limited — RECOMMEND

- Honest acquisition improvement: turn the current visually attractive but platform-shaped page into an owned salon proposition that surfaces core hair/extensions/beauty categories and proof before booking.
- Static feasibility: Hero, three stable service groups, selected gallery-led proof, location/contact and a single external booking CTA; timetable, policies, customer login and service availability stay on Ovatu.
- Real public basis: official hero/logo, official gallery, current reviews, public address/email, official Instagram and matching local listing.
- GBP600 rationale: 411 reviews, a branded physical venue, multi-provider delivery and existing booking investment make a one-time acquisition page proportionate without inferring turnover.
- Risk control: keep the page evergreen and do not reproduce changing timetables, availability or the full treatment catalogue.
- Verdict: `RECOMMEND`.
