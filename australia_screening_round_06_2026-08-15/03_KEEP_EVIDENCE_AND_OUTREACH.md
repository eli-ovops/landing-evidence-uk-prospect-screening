### Two reproducible display/enquiry problems

1. **Residual/undermining text:** open [home](https://www.thomasstewart.com.au/) and find the literal line `check subliminal message at bottom of page`; then open [contact](https://www.thomasstewart.com.au/contact/) and find “This is a subliminal message that you will read…”. Both are public and can weaken professional clarity at the conversion point.
2. **Price/next-step discontinuity:** home contains a `Pricing` section but only says to contact for a full package list; the separate [prices page](https://www.thomasstewart.com.au/prices-packages/) exposes only “around $5k” and hours, again requiring contact for the actual list. A visitor must traverse pages and initiate contact before understanding a simple offer boundary.

### Static-page scope and Demo direction (description only)

**Can contain:** one hero proposition; short “warm + joyous documentary” positioning; three stable coverage bands described without live availability; selected licensed/approved portfolio tiles; simple process; Bowral/Southern Highlands credibility; `mailto:` / phone / official Instagram.

**Must exclude:** date/availability calendar; form collection; package-list downloads; contracts/deposits/payment; client gallery/delivery; dynamically updated blog/feed; any unapproved images/testimonials.

**Demo direction:** “A calm Bowral wedding-photography introduction: three concise coverage pathways, a transparent ‘typical investment’ context, a small approved work selection, and one direct email CTA.” Do not make it unless separately authorised and assets/claims are cleared.

### AU$800 case, strongest red team, and decision

- **Support:** Existing average customer spend is publicly stated near AU$5k; the static page would not replace any workflow, only reduce journey/copy friction before existing direct contact.
- **Against:** The current visual design may already be contemporary; price information may be intentionally withheld; the writer’s playful “subliminal” copy may be deliberate brand voice. No evidence proves lost enquiries or willingness to spend AU$800.
- **Falsifiable go/no-go:** **GO only for PMO deep review** if an ordinary browser confirms the residual copy and fragmented price/CTA journey on desktop and mobile, and the owner later confirms a static introductory page is wanted. **NO-GO** if browser rendering shows these are non-visible/intentional or a current high-conversion landing page already exists.
- **Result:** **KEEP P1 — PMO review candidate**, not Demo/outreach-authorised.
- **Evidence gaps:** no verified owner authority/ABN, mobile browser QA, live enquiry test, actual conversion data, asset rights, valid email deliverability, consent/lawful basis, or budget acceptance.

- **Evidence gaps:** no direct official-IG recency, mobile interaction, AU$800 acceptance, owner authority, current/published-price confirmation, email deliverability, consent/lawful basis, or asset/testimonial permission.
