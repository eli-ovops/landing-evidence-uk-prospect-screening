One local operator, many ways to travel

Transport that fits the occasion, the landscape and your group.

From winery and pub tours to weddings, snow transfers, school trips, cycling trails and private group travel, Bright & District Leisure Tours offers a range of buses and flexible travel options.

All buses are air conditioned, heated and fitted with seatbelts. Drivers are licensed, and the company is fully accredited for passenger transport.

Explore every service

Bus tours and group transport

Choose a starting point. Shape the day around your people.

Every service below is drawn from the current public Bright Bus Tours offering.

01Pub and winery tours+

Explore local pubs, wineries, craft breweries, orchards, distilleries, cafes and restaurants. Half-day tours can include the Happy Valley Pub, Railway Hotel Wandi Pub, Harrietville Pub, Bright pubs and Porepunkah Pub. Build your own tour with places you would like to visit.

02Wedding bus transport+

Wedding transport can collect guests from the wedding party location, accommodation or venue, travel to photo locations and the reception, then provide return transport.

03Sporting events and club outings+

Group transport is available for sporting events, club outings and social groups.

04School and educational trips+

School trips and educational excursions are part of the public service offering. The current site states that buses are fully compliant and drivers have the relevant licences, background checks and Working With Children Checks.

05Hiking and cycling trails+

Pickup and drop-off transport is available for hiking and cycling groups across Mt Buffalo, Mt Hotham, Mt Bogong and Falls Creek. The public site welcomes groups from two people to 30 or more.

06Family holidays and private tours+

Private transport and tours are available for couples, small families and private parties, including group travel during a family holiday.

07Airport transfers+

Airport transfers are listed for Melbourne Tullamarine, Albury and Mt Hotham airports, for groups of any size and at any time.

08Sightseeing tours+

Discover the Victorian High Country with sightseeing tours for groups.

09Corporate events+

Corporate transport can support airport and accommodation transfers, country events, educational trips, site visits, client entertainment, Christmas parties and team-building activities.

10Bespoke bus tours+

Create a bus tour around your own group, interests and itinerary. The company invites customers to discuss a bespoke tour.

11Snow and ski transfers+

Over-snow and ski transfers are available. The current public site states that drivers hold hazardous-conditions licences for snow travel.

12Other group travel+

Other group travel and transportation requirements can be discussed directly with Bright & District Leisure Tours.
