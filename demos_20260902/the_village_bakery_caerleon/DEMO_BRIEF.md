# Demo brief — The Village Bakery and Cafe

Date: 2026-09-02. Status: local Concept Demo only; no deployment, upload, outreach or source-transfer authorization.

## Audience and first-screen job

The audience is the owner of The Village Bakery and Cafe, Caerleon. In one glance, the page must make a local owner recognise a warm bakery-and-cafe direction, see a clearer visit path than fragmented directory/social listings, and understand that the page can be a finished static first site.

## Evidence-led scope

Verified: bakery and cafe identity; Caerleon address; telephone; listed hours; breads, pastries/cakes and sourdough as listing descriptors; takeaway, cafe visit and event catering as listing claims. No live menu, price, stock, booking, ordering, award, staff identity, shopfront or official visual is claimed. Customer authorization: NOT VERIFIED.

## Page hierarchy

1. Hero: named place, handmade bakery/cafe proposition, single phone CTA.
2. Three offer cues: bread, pastry, cafe visit.
3. Craft vignette: original concept visual plus enduring service language.
4. Visit card: verified address/hours/telephone, with an explicit check-hours caveat.
5. Closing: a quiet Caerleon-led invitation and repeat phone CTA.

## CTA and non-scope

Primary CTA is `tel:+441633439500`; it does not assert bookings, ordering or availability. The static slice excludes menu, ecommerce, payment, booking, account/login, CMS, inventory, delivery promise, customer data capture, deployment and ongoing maintenance.
