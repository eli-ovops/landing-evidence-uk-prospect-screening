## Design intent, dials and alternatives

**Design Read:** A brand-register, locally grounded bakery/cafe single page for a proprietor who needs a clear first owned presence, shaped as a warm editorial invitation rather than a generic menu template.

- `SOUL 8/10`: hearth-and-hedgerow—ink green, butter paper, weathered brass, tactile grain.
- `SPECTACLE 3/10`: a still image-led hero with subtle entrance only; no automatic animation.
- `DENSITY 6/10`: compact proof/visit detail, generous but not empty reading room.

Rejected lazy defaults: white card-grid cafe site, generic brunch collage, oversized “book now” panel, fake menu and fabricated review strip.

Direction A (selected): **The Bakehouse Ledger** — full-bleed hands-and-sourdough hero; an asymmetrical pastry craft block; a dark local visit ledger; a cinematic lane close. It makes a fragmented business feel owned and deliberate.

Direction B (rejected): **The Morning Room** — a light, typographic cafe journal with a large local map-like visit panel and food still-life inserts. It was more literary but less immediately recognisable as bakery craft.
