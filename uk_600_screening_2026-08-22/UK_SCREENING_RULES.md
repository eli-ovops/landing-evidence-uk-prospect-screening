## Continuous-pool rule v1.2 — 2026-08-22

There is no discovery-pool ceiling. If fewer than three current all-hard-gate passers exist after a 20-object batch, start the next neutral batch automatically. A completed batch, 60/100/140-object total, or any other count is never a blocking condition.

Every newly discovered row must receive a recorded H0 investigation before it can say `H0 NOT VERIFIED`: public sources checked, direct URL, evidence date (or explicit absence of a qualifying date), result and failure reason. A discovery listing alone is not an H0 investigation and cannot close a row. H0 PASS proceeds through Track, H0.5, public email, H1–H6, dual-viewport review and scoring; inaccessible evidence remains HOLD.

## Product under test

- Fixed first-order validation price: **£600**.
- A polished static one-page site: HTML, CSS and limited front-end JavaScript only.
- May link to an existing third-party booking service, social profile, phone number or business email.
- Excludes CMS, admin, database, login, self-hosted booking, ecommerce, payments, inventory, server-side forms and ongoing content maintenance.
- Domain, hosting, tax and future maintenance: **TBC; not included by default**.

## Eligibility sequence

`H0 current trading → website status/Track → H0.5 meaningful static-demo opportunity → public-business-email gate → H1–H6 → browser evidence → 100-point prioritisation score`.

- H0 needs a dated, attributable public trading signal in the 90-day window 2026-05-24 through 2026-08-22.
- Track N requires three recorded current search surfaces. Track W requires an attributable owned site and desktop plus approximately 390px mobile observation.
- `£600 Purchase Signal` is evidence of commercial capacity, recent investment, higher-value work, or customer-acquisition value. It is never a purchase or conversion prediction.
- Public business email must be visibly published by an attributable public business source, with source URL and retrieval date. Phone, form and social DM never substitute. Deliverability remains `UNKNOWN` where no individual mailbox validator was available.
- A final candidate needs every hard gate PASS. The score only orders hard-gate passers; it cannot repair a missing gate.
